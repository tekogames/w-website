Description

Sara Himura has just come aboard the THK58, a ship bound for a distant colony. The journey will not go as expected. Play as Sara and uncover the secrets of the THK58, and of Sara herself.

Wraithmind: Escape the THK58 blends visual novel storytelling with third-person combat. Talk your way through the crew members, sneak into places you're not supposed to be, and fight when talking stops working, all while uncovering a truth that hits closer to home than Sara expects.

Features

  • Branching dialogue and a cast of crew with their own goals and secrets
  • Explore the THK58 one location at a time as the story unfolds
  • Third-person combat sequences when things go wrong
  • A mystery that's as much about Sara as it is about the ship

History

Wraithmind: Escape the THK58 is a spinoff of Wraithmind, the science fiction turn-based RPG released by TEKO in 2020. The new game is set in the same universe and is being developed by OxenTiger in collaboration with TEKO.
