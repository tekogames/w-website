The Wraithmind universe

Humanity has just reached for the stars. Something reached back.

Wraithmind is a series of science fiction games set in the 22nd century, in an age when humans have only begun to explore the stars. Starships cross the void to Alpha Centauri, mining outposts grow into colonies, and governments race to claim what lies beyond Earth.

Out on the frontier, power is held by those who control the research. Rival blocs stand locked in a cold war, secret laboratories run experiments no one is meant to hear about, and ancient ruins on a distant moon hold answers that were never meant to be found. Each game follows a different person pulled into the same story, from a woman who wakes with powers she cannot explain to a young traveler whose quiet voyage is anything but.

  • The year2118Interstellar travel is new, and so is the fear that comes with it.
  • The frontierTauriaA moon in the Alpha Centauri system, with a mining station and ruins older than humanity.
  • The powersThe GCA and the UNRTwo rival blocs, a cold war, and rebels caught in between.
  • The mysteryThe WraithmindA voice from far beyond the stars, calling to be found.

Play them in any order. Every game stands on its own, and each one reveals more of the whole.
